# Core Business Questions — Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

The primary objective of this project is to analyze customer data, diagnose why and when customers leave, and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 1. Overall Churn & Baseline Health
- What is the total customer count, total churned customers, and overall churn rate for the telecom provider?
- How does the churn rate compare across key demographics (gender, senior citizen status, partners, dependents)?

## 2. Contract & Commitment Analysis
- How does the customer churn rate vary between **Month-to-month**, **One-year**, and **Two-year** contract types?
- What proportion of total churners belong to short-term vs. long-term contracts?

## 3. Customer Tenure & Lifecycle Trends
- At what stage in the customer lifecycle is attrition most acute (e.g., within 0-6 months vs. 2+ years)?
- What is the median tenure of churned customers compared to retained customers?

## 4. Product, Service & Add-on Stickiness
- How does churn differ between **Fiber optic**, **DSL**, and **No internet** customers?
- Do security and support services (e.g., `TechSupport`, `OnlineSecurity`) correlate with higher customer retention?
- How do bundled streaming services affect customer stickiness?

## 5. Pricing & Financial Sensitivity
- Are customers paying higher monthly bills (`MonthlyCharges`) more prone to leaving?
- What is the churn rate across monthly spending brackets (e.g., low < $35, medium $35-$70, high > $70)?

## 6. Payment Channel & Friction
- Which payment methods exhibit the highest attrition rate?
- Does automatic billing (`Bank transfer`, `Credit card`) show significantly better customer retention than manual payment (`Electronic check`, `Mailed check`)?

## 7. High-Risk Customer Profiling & Retention Action
- Which combined customer profile represents the highest risk of churn (e.g., Month-to-month + Fiber Optic + Electronic Check + High Monthly Charges)?
- What revenue is at risk, and what targeted, testable retention campaigns should leadership implement?
